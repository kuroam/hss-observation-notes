# 00. Translation Policy

The Japanese version is the canonical source.

The English version is a working translation / adaptation.
English terminology is provisional.

If there is ambiguity or conflict, the Japanese concept takes priority.

The English version must not introduce new HSS concepts that do not exist in the Japanese version.

HSS should be read as:

- a provisional observation memo
- a proof-of-concept observation model
- a coarse-grained structural scanner

HSS should not be read as:

- a completed theory
- a doctrine
- a personality typology
- a theory of human nature
- an ethical system
- a theory of emotion
- a theory of neurophysiology
- a theory of AI consciousness
