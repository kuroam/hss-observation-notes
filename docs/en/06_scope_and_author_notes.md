# 06. Scope and Author Notes

## 1. Purpose of this document

This document organizes the observation scope of HSS, reserved layers, explanatory power, truth-value, connection confirmation precision, and author comments.

The Japanese source is canonical. English terminology is provisional.

## 2. Scope handled by HSS

HSS is not a fixed theory. It is a PoC for observing and decomposing connection structures.

HSS is not a theory for completely explaining humans or the world. It is a structural memo for observing, at a coarse grain, connection structures that may appear in culture, relationships, creative works, markets, organizations, platforms, symbols, and related domains.

In the current version, HSS mainly handles L1–L6 connection structures. These include routes and states such as continuous connection, reconnection, re-expansion, fixation, Blue residuals, reconnectable difference, synchronization pressure, compression, fixed-symbol circulation, layered history, stratified routing, feedback deficit, and objective function capture.

L1–L6 are provisional placements for observation, not complete layer definitions.

## 3. L7/L8 and reserved layers

L7/L8 are not outside HSS.

Emotion, bodily desire, neurophysiological response, passion, impulse, pleasure, pain, desire for recognition, and related phenomena may be positioned around L7/L8.

HSS does not model them directly. This is not because HSS treats them as unimportant or ignores them. Rather, because emotion, desire, embodiment, and neurophysiology are too consequential to model casually, HSS does not incorporate them too easily into the main body of a coarse-grained structural observation memo.

HSS handles traces that appear in L1–L6 as reactions, traces, symbols, processing forms, or routing.

At the current stage, L7/L8 are treated as reserved layers whose traces may be observed only through L1–L6 connection structures.

## 4. Explanatory power, truth-value, and connection confirmation precision

HSS may describe or organize a phenomenon at a certain grain size. This does not mean objective truth.

HSS is an observation coordinate system, not a truth system. Seeing a structure from one observation coordinate system and that structure being objectively correct are different matters.

HSS does not treat decomposition results as success or failure.

Results can be treated as connection confirmation states, such as:

- confirmed connection
- unconfirmed connection
- different connection destination
- absorption into a processing form
- shortage of traces
- out-of-scope layer
- shortage of information

These states do not mean that the target has no value or that the observation target is denied. They mean that, at the observational grain size of HSS, the connection structure should be recorded with that level of confirmation.

If connection source, connection destination, mediating symbol, processing form, traces, routing, or reconnection route cannot be separated, the result should remain low precision or pending.

## 5. Distance from nihilism and stratified routing

HSS may decompose meaning and value structurally, but it does not deny meaning or value.

HSS is not a decomposer for denying meaning or value. It is a structural memo for observing where the connection routes that support meaning and value may be weakening, being compressed, being captured, or about to be cut off.

Stratified routing refers to connection routes that include `Shiwa` — lived creases or frictional traces — formed by experience, memory, culture, civilization, relationships, discomfort, and continuous connection.

The stronger structural decomposition becomes, the more important stratified routing becomes. The author personally treats preservation of stratified routing as important, but this is not an axiom of HSS.

## 6. Safety principles and provisional conclusion

When using HSS, the following points are assumed:

- Being able to describe or organize something does not mean being correct.
- Being able to decompose something as structure does not mean that it has no meaning.
- The explanatory power of a model is not proof of objective truth.
- HSS is not a finished theory, but an observation memo that may be updated.
- Do not casually model L7/L8.
- Do not classify humans into fixed types.
- Do not reduce stratified routing to mere efficiency or indicators.
- Do not confuse the observation function of the main body of HSS with the author’s statement of position.

HSS does not define how humans should be. It is a PoC for observing how connection structures appear, change, are lost, and are reconnected.
