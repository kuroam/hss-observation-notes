# 04. Market Optimization

This memo treats not markets or optimization themselves, but high-speed circulation structures in short-cycle markets and their relationship with cultural formation and layered history / stratified history, as observation targets.

This memo lightly observes relationships such as the following as connection structures.

- High-speed recursion
- Fixed symbol circulation
- Reconnectable areas
- Layered history / stratified history formation

## High-Speed Circulation Structures

In short-cycle markets, structures such as the following may operate strongly.

- Low cost
- High-speed sharing
- High recursion
- Strong diffusibility

Through these structures, information sharing, trend formation, and connection formation may become faster.

## Fixed Symbol Circulation

In high-speed recursive structures, the following kinds of things may become easier to circulate repeatedly.

- Fixed symbols
- Strong meanings
- Low-tokenized connections

As a result, high-speed sharing, reusability, and low-cost circulation may be strengthened.

## Relationship with Layered History / Stratified History

In HSS, cultural formation is provisionally placed as a process in which connections and Flow accumulate over time.

For this reason, when states such as the following become biased, their possible effects on layered history / stratified history formation are treated as observation targets.

- High-speed fixed symbol circulation
- Shrinking of reconnectable areas
- Reduction of room for reinterpretation

## Overemphasis

Here, the observation target is not speed or optimization itself, but how connection structures appear to change when an overemphasis on high-speed circulation of fixed symbols occurs.

The following properties may be observed as structures that accelerate connection formation.

- High-speed sharing
- Recursiveness
- Speed of connection formation

In HSS, when an overemphasis on high-speed circulation of fixed symbols occurs, the memo observes what kinds of changes may be seen in the following.

- Reconnectable areas
- Blue residuals
- Layered history / stratified history formation

## Feedback Deficit

In HSS, states in which negative feedback may be weakened in short-cycle markets or platform indicators are also treated as observation targets.

For example, food, money, power, and bodily burden may generate some kind of rebound or corrective feedback when they become excessive.

By contrast, recognition, views, followers, and short-term reactions may more easily continue as positive feedback, and rebound or corrective feedback may be harder to observe.

In this case, while short-term rewards and platform indicators are strengthened, layered history / stratified history and stratified routing may become thinner.

In HSS, this state is provisionally treated as a “feedback deficit.”

Here, this memo observes not recognition or market evaluation themselves, but how short-term rewards and indicators appear to strengthen some connection routes and weaken others.

## Relationship with Objective Function Capture

In HSS, objective function capture and feedback deficit are separate terms, while they may often be observed together.

For example, when external indicators such as views, followers, KPIs, and evaluation metrics begin to be treated as if they were the objectives of oneself or an organization, routing initiative may become externalized.

At this point, rebound or corrective feedback that would otherwise occur, such as discomfort, fatigue, reassessment, and renewed distancing, may be weakened, and only positive feedback may more easily remain.

However, HSS does not treat this as a simple one-directional causal relationship.

Objective function capture may strengthen feedback deficit, and feedback deficit may make objective function capture easier.

Here, the two are observed as structures that may easily operate together in short-term rewards and market indicators.

## Treatment

This memo is not intended to define markets or culture.

It is a light observational memo for organizing how high-speed circulation structures and layered history / stratified history formation may appear as connection structures.
